### 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Data Scientist & ML Engineer** from 🇱🇰 Sri Lanka, passionate about extracting intelligence from complex datasets. My work spans statistical modeling, deep learning, natural language processing, and building end-to-end data pipelines.

- 🔬 Researching **causal inference** and **multivariate statistical analysis**
- 🤖 Building **NLP** and **deep learning** models with TensorFlow, Keras & PyTorch
- 📊 Experienced in statistical tools: **Minitab**, **SPSS**, **Octave**, **R**
- 🗄️ Skilled in **database management** and **big data analysis**
- 🚀 Currently working on the **Sri Lanka Car Market Analysis Dashboard**
