## About Me

I am a Final Year Data Science Undergraduate at the University of Colombo with a strong interest in data-driven decision making, machine learning, and statistical analysis. My academic and project experience spans data visualization, predictive modeling, business intelligence, and analytical research.

### Areas of Interest

- 📊 Data Visualization & Business Intelligence (Power BI, Excel)
- 🤖 Machine Learning & Deep Learning
- 💬 Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- 📈 Statistical Inference & Causal Inference
- 📉 Time Series Analysis & Forecasting
- 🔍 Multivariate Data Analysis
- 🗄️ Database Management & SQL
- 🌐 Big Data Analytics

### Currently Learning

- Advanced Deep Learning Architectures
- Transformer Models & NLP Applications
- MLOps & CI/CD for Machine Learning
- Data Engineering Fundamentals
- Cloud-Based Analytics Solutions

### Career Goal

To leverage data science, machine learning, and analytics to solve real-world problems and create impactful, data-driven solutions.
